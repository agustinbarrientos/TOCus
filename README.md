### Pause before visiting addictive websites

TOCus adds a short pause before the websites you choose, giving you a moment to breathe and decide what to do next.

[Get TOCus](https://tocus.uo.ar/) for desktop Chrome, Edge, and Firefox.

[![CI](https://github.com/agustinbarrientos/TOCus/actions/workflows/ci.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/agustinbarrientos/TOCus/actions/workflows/ci.yml) [![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-MIT-blue.svg)](LICENSE)

</div>

## Features

- Choose your websites and customize pause and browsing times.
- Set active days and hours, with separate schedules for each site.
- Automatically pause videos while you take a breath.
- See completed pauses, reconsidered visits, and estimated time saved.
- Choose colors, light and dark themes, and optional breathing guidance.
- Use TOCus in ten languages and language variants.

The initial pause lasts 10 seconds. By default, you get 5 minutes to browse when you continue.

## Privacy

Free and open source. No accounts, ads, or tracking. TOCus works offline; settings and statistics stay on your device. Statistics store counts and durations, without page content or web addresses. An active pause may temporarily keep its destination address so you can return to the website.

You control website access and can delete your data in settings. Read the [privacy policy](https://tocus.uo.ar/privacy/).
