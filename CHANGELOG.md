# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release of TOCus for desktop Chrome, Microsoft Edge, and Firefox.

- A breathing pause before selected websites, with customizable pause and browsing times.
- Active hours and separate schedules for individual websites.
- Automatic video pausing during a breathing pause.
- A toolbar popup for adding websites and checking their current status.
- Local statistics for completed pauses, reconsidered visits, and estimated time saved.
- Six colors, light and dark themes, optional breathing guidance, and support for the system's reduced-motion preference.
- Guided setup and settings in ten languages and language variants.
- Controls to review website permissions, reset statistics, and remove local data.
- Offline operation with no accounts, ads, or tracking.
